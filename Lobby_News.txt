FIGHTER ACE RESURRECTION - server update, 17 September
This page is new: news now comes from the server, 
and admins can post here.


TERRITORIAL COMBAT
Bases are easier to trigger: buildings now take their historical damage, 
not 5x.
Factories, hangars, HQs and bridges need bombs or rockets - 
guns won't do it.
AA, flak, fuel tanks, storage, towers and barracks can still be strafed.
Craters last 10 minutes and everyone sees the same holes in the runway.
Repairs take four times longer, and a base under assault does not 
repair at all.
Paratroops must land complete and hold a scene for 90 seconds to 
capture it.
Infantry now shoot back at AA and flak instead of walking into them.
Tank columns form at the nearest supplied base, not a 
factory 20 km away.
Each nation now fields its own tanks and infantry.


MAP
Tanks, paratroops and supply trains now show on the map with 
strength and orders.
Three supply trains per nation run the rails - rear, middle and front.


SCORES
Squadron and pilot boards now tally buildings, tanks, ground units, 
trains and captures.
Kills are credited only on real evidence - no more phantom 
or double kills.
Repositioning with TAB no longer costs you a plane and a pilot.
